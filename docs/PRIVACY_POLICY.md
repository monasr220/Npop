## 2. Data we collect

### Account data

- Username, email address, bio and optional contact information.
- Password. Passwords are stored only as salted, one-way hashes (Argon2id where
  available, with a secure fallback). We never store or display your plain-text
  password.
- Account role (listener, artist or administrator), timestamps such as account
  creation, last login and last update.

### Content you create

- Posts, comments, likes, ratings, reposts and hashtags.
- Playlists, favorites, profile tracks, follows and listening history (the
  tracks you play while signed in).
- Notifications generated by activity on your account.

### Artist and media data

- Artist profile name, biography, image, accent preference and verification
  status.
- Albums, track metadata (title, duration, track number, explicit flag) and the
  audio or artwork you upload or link.
- Profile pictures, cover photos and social links you add.

### Technical data

- Authentication tokens issued by the API and stored by your browser so you stay
  signed in until the token expires or you sign out.
- Server logs that may include IP address, request path, user agent, timestamps
  and error details, used for security, debugging and abuse prevention.

## 3. How we use data

- Create and secure your account, authenticate sessions and enforce permissions.
- Provide discovery, playback, playlists, favorites and listening history.
- Publish artist profiles, releases and uploaded music.
- Deliver the social feed, comments, ratings, follows and notifications.
- Moderate content, verify artists and investigate abuse or security incidents.
- Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the reliability of the service.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your data for third-party
advertising, and the current codebase does not include third-party analytics or
advertising SDKs.

## 4. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the
following, as applicable: performance of a contract with you (providing the
service), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, service
improvement), your consent (where we ask for it), and compliance with legal
obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis,
without affecting processing that already took place.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

Npop does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The web client uses browser
local storage for essential preferences and session state:

- `npop_token` — the authentication token that keeps you signed in.
- `npop_theme` — your dark or light theme preference.
- `npop_volume` — your player volume preference.
- `npop_shuffle`, `npop_repeat`, `npop_eq` — player preferences.

Clearing site data in your browser signs you out and resets these preferences.
Server-side sessions are represented by signed tokens with an expiry time.

## 6. Sharing and processors

We share data only as needed to run the service, with providers that process it
on our instructions. Depending on configuration, this may include:

- Hosting and infrastructure providers that run the application, database and
  logs.
- Media storage and delivery providers (for example Cloudinary) that store
  uploaded audio, artwork and profile images.
- Professional advisers, auditors or authorities where required by law, or to
  protect the rights, safety and security of Npop and its users.

We require processors to protect data and to use it only for the agreed purpose.
We do not sell personal data.

## 7. Retention

We keep account and content data while your account is active and for a
reasonable period afterwards where needed for backups, dispute resolution,
security or legal compliance. When you delete your account, associated profile
data, posts, playlists, favorites, listening history and artist content are
deleted from the active database, subject to backup rotation and legal retention
requirements. Server logs are kept for [LOG RETENTION PERIOD] unless a longer
period is required for an investigation.

## 8. Security

Npop applies technical and organizational safeguards, including hashed
passwords, expiring signed authentication tokens, server-side authorization
checks, request rate limiting, input validation, upload validation and security
headers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot
guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised,
contact us immediately at [SECURITY CONTACT EMAIL].

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or
delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to
withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection
authority. Signed-in users can update profile details and delete their account
from Settings. To exercise other rights, contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. We may
need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

## 10. Children

Npop is not directed to children below the minimum age required in your
jurisdiction (for example, 13 in the United States or 16 in some regions). We do
not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe
a child has provided data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to
delete it.
